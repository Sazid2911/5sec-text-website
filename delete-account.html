Delete your 5 Sec Text account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open 5 Sec Text and tap the ⚙ Settings icon.
  2. Tap Withdraw consent & delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email fivesectext@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, display name and username - deleted immediately.
  • Messages you sent that have not been read yet - deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • Read messages are already deleted, so nothing else remains.
  • Your deals and videos end and are removed.
  • Kept for 1 year as Indian law requires, then deleted automatically: the minimal activity log (sign-ins, account and post records - never message content) and complaints or reports you filed.
