Privacy by design

Nothing stays behind

Every part of the app is built so a conversation leaves no trace on the phone or on our servers.

Gone 5 seconds after reading

Texts and photos count down from 5 once opened, then are deleted from the phone and the server. Voice and video play once. Unread messages expire after 7 days.

No screenshots or recording

Android blocks screenshots, screen recording and the recent-apps preview inside 5 Sec Text. There is no save or download button anywhere.

A blank home screen

No chat list and no names. When everything is read the screen is empty. New messages appear as anonymous rows until you open them.

End-to-end encrypted

Messages are locked on your phone (X25519 key exchange with AES-256-GCM) and only the people in the chat can open them. We cannot read them.

Camera only, nothing attached

Photos and videos are taken live in the app. Nobody can forward files from their gallery, so what you see is what was just captured.

Email sign-in, no phone number

Sign in with a one-time link sent to your email. The app checks that an active SIM is in the phone but never reads your number.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I get a message back after it disappears?

No. Once a message has been read and its 5 seconds are up, it is deleted from both phones and from our servers. That is the point of the app.

Does 5 Sec Text cost anything?

No. Signing up and messaging are free. Sign-in uses a one-time email link, so there are no SMS charges.

Why does it need a SIM card if it doesn't use my number?

An active SIM helps keep out throwaway and bot accounts. The app only checks that a SIM is present; it never reads or sends your phone number.

Are there voice or video calls?

No, by design. 5 Sec Text is for text, photos, voice notes and short videos that disappear.

Will I get notified when the app is closed?

In this first version, alerts arrive while the app is open or running in the background. Notifications show only "New message", never names or text.

How do shops post a deal?

Open the Deals tab, tap Post a deal, add the discount, code and an optional picture, and confirm the offer is genuine and lawful. It goes live at once and ends on the date you choose.
