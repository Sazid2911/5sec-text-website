Privacy Policy

5 Sec Text · Last updated: 27 November 2026

5 Sec Text ("the app", "we") is a messaging app built around one idea: messages should not stay around. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how long we keep it.

Notice and consent

Before you create an account, the app shows an itemised notice of the data below and why we need it, and asks for your separate consent (as well as your 18+ confirmation and acceptance of the Terms). You can read the notice any time in Settings → Your data & consent. You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, in Settings → Withdraw consent & delete account: because the app cannot work without this data, withdrawing deletes your account, videos and deals immediately.

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, your optional mobile number (stored only as a scrambled code) (you sign in with your email and a password handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see your password), your display name and username, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older and accepted the Terms of Use (with the date).
  • Messages: encrypted text, photos, voice messages and videos (we only store the encrypted form), plus who sent them, to which chat, when, and who has read them.
  • Encryption public key: needed so others can send you encrypted messages.
  • Safety data: the list of people you block, reports you submit, complaints you file with our Grievance Officer (and our replies), and any suspension or ban on your account.
  • Minimal activity log: see "Activity log" below.

We do not upload your contact list, and we do not collect your location (the "Nearest shops" sort uses your location on your phone only; vendors may choose to pin their shop's location on a deal), advertising ID, IP address logs or browsing data. A mobile number is optional (see below). The app checks on your phone that an active SIM card is inserted; no SIM details are sent to us. Photos and videos can be taken with the in-app camera or picked from your gallery when you choose to; the app only reads the item you pick. The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics.

How long messages are kept

  • A message is permanently deleted from our servers as soon as every recipient has read it (texts and photos: 5 seconds after they are opened by default, or the time the reader chooses in Settings, up to 30 seconds; voice messages: after they finish playing once).
  • Messages that are never read are automatically deleted after 7 days.
  • Photos and voice messages are stored only inside the message itself, so they are deleted at the same moment.

Status (photos and videos for 24 hours)

Status photos and videos are stored only on your own phone, never on our servers. When a contact views your status, it is sent directly from your phone to theirs over an encrypted peer-to-peer connection; our servers only help the two phones find each other. If a direct route is blocked by the mobile network, the encrypted stream may pass through a public relay (TURN) server, which cannot read it. We store only a small card for each update (your display name, optional caption, photo or video, expiry) and a list of which of your status slots are in use; cards are deleted automatically after 24 hours. Your status is visible only to your contacts: people you chat with, phone contacts who use 5 Sec Text (if you allowed contact matching) and people you add; you can remove anyone in "Who can see my status". Likes, replies and views are sent to you as end-to-end encrypted direct messages, relayed and deleted from our servers at once, and kept only on your phone. Viewers' copies are deleted when they close the status. Every photo and video is checked for nudity on your phone before posting. Peer-to-peer connections can reveal your phone's IP address to the viewer, as with any direct connection.

Offline Bluetooth chat

Text messages sent with "Offline · Bluetooth" go directly between two nearby phones. Nothing passes through or is stored on our servers.

Finding friends from your contacts (optional)

  • Your number: you may add your mobile number in Settings so people who have it saved can find you. We store only a scrambled code of it (a one-way SHA-256 hash), linked to your account, never the number itself. It is not verified by SMS, so the app always shows your @username to the person who finds you. Remove it at any time in Settings; it is also removed when you delete your account.
  • Your contacts: only if you allow it, the app reads the names and numbers in your phone's contacts on your phone. Your contact list is never uploaded or stored by us. To check which contacts use 5 Sec Text, the app sends only the scrambled code of each number and receives back whether an account added that number. We keep no record of these checks.
  • Usernames you link to contacts: you can link a friend's @username to a contact saved in your phone. These links are stored only on your phone, in encrypted storage, and are never sent to us.
  • Note: like any phone-number lookup, a scrambled code of a phone number could in theory be matched by someone who tries every possible number, so add your number only if you want to be findable this way.

Offline Bluetooth chat

When there is no internet, you can send text messages to a nearby 5 Sec Text user over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct. These messages go directly between the two phones, never through our servers, are not stored, and disappear 5 seconds after they are seen. Android requires Bluetooth and (on older versions) location permission to find nearby phones; your location is not collected or shared.

The app has no voice or video calling.

Activity log (kept 1 year)

Indian law requires us to keep basic records so we can respond to lawful orders from courts and authorities. We keep only the minimum, for 1 year, after which each entry is deleted automatically:

  • Sign-ins and app opens: your user ID, sign-in email and the time.
  • Account records: account created or deleted (with your username), and when you accepted the Terms and confirmed you are 18+.
  • Post records: when you post, update or end a video or a deal, with its title (for deals also the shop name, discount and your "genuine and lawful" confirmation).
  • Complaints: when you file one.

The log never contains message content, photos, videos, voice, contacts or location, and we do not record your IP address. You cannot edit or delete log entries, and only our Trust & Safety staff can read them, only to handle a complaint or a legal order. Staff actions (look-ups, removals, bans) are logged too. The log and complaint records are kept for 1 year even if you delete your account, then deleted.

Complaints, removals and bans

Vendors and uploaders manage their own posts. Our small Trust & Safety team acts only on complaints and on orders from courts or authorised government agencies: it may remove a deal or video, send you a notice, or suspend or ban an account. You are told in the app and can appeal. Complaints are kept for 1 year.

How we use your data

Only to deliver your messages, let people find you by username, keep the service safe (blocking and reports), and prevent abuse. We do not sell or share your personal data with anyone for marketing.

Where data is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), operated by Google LLC. All messages, photos, voice messages and videos are end-to-end encrypted on your phone before they are sent (X25519 key exchange + AES-256-GCM); only the people in the chat can decrypt them. We cannot read your messages. Each phone's private key never leaves the device.

Screenshots and downloads

The app blocks screenshots and screen recording on Android, hides its content in the recent-apps screen, and has no option to save or download photos. The home screen shows no names or chat list. It cannot stop someone photographing their screen with another device, so only send things to people you trust.

Your choices and rights

  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Withdraw consent & delete account, or by following the steps on our account deletion page.
  • You can block anyone and report messages or users from the chat menu.
  • Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask us for a summary of your personal data and how we use it, ask us to correct or erase it, withdraw consent (by deleting your account), and nominate a person to exercise these rights if you die or cannot act. Write to the Grievance Officer below. Data we must keep by law (the activity log and complaints) is erased when its 1-year period ends.
  • If a personal data breach happens, we will inform affected users and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

Adults only (18+)

5 Sec Text is only for people aged 18 or older. Everyone confirms this when signing up. We do not knowingly collect data of anyone under 18, and we delete accounts we find belong to a child.
